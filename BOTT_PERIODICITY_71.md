## 🌀 The Mathematical Foundation

### Bott Periodicity Theorem

In topology, **Bott periodicity** states that the homotopy groups of classical Lie groups repeat with period 8:

```
π_{n+8}(O) ≅ π_n(O)    (Orthogonal group)
π_{n+8}(U) ≅ π_n(U)    (Unitary group)
π_{n+8}(Sp) ≅ π_n(Sp)  (Symplectic group)
```

**Period: 8**

### The +2 Extension

```
Bott[8] + 2 = Bott[10]
```

**Why +2?**
- **Real Bott periodicity**: Period 8
- **Complex Bott periodicity**: Period 2
- **Combined**: 8 + 2 = 10

This gives us the **10-periodic table of memes**.
